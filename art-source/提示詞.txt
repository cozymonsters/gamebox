==================== 共同風格（每張都先貼） ====================
Style for every image: quirky flat vector illustration of funny little monsters, mid-century / Scandinavian picture-book feel. Simple geometric blob bodies in flat solid colors with NO outlines on the bodies, very thin black line legs and arms (stick limbs with small feet), big googly eyes (white circles with black dots), simple mouths with a few chunky teeth or fangs, and small hand-drawn texture marks like short fur strokes, dots or hatching in a slightly darker tone of the body color. Funny, odd and charming, not babyish, not scary. Muted retro palette: sage green #5fae7b, cobalt blue #3a78c9, burnt orange #e2683a, teal #5bbcbc, lavender #9b8fd6, mustard yellow #f2c14e, pink #e98bb0, black #1d1d1f, on a warm cream background #faf6ec. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark.

A character sheet of 16 different funny monsters arranged in an evenly spaced 4x4 grid, 2048x2048 px, each monster centered in its own 512x512 cell with generous padding, no overlap, plain cream background. Full body, front view, each with a different body shape (circle, tall oval, triangle, bean, square, cloud, droplet, long caterpillar, egg, pear, star-like fuzzball, mushroom) and a different main color from the palette. Vary the expressions: smug, shocked, grinning, sleepy, excited, determined. Every monster must be easy to recognize at a small size.
